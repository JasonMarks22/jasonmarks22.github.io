About

I love learning how things work, and then using what I learn to solve real problems.

The work I want to do is in software and data: building tools people use, designing systems that run reliably, and digging into data to find answers. I'm looking for a company where I can start my career and make a real difference by solving the problems that keep the business moving forward.

To get there, I build on my own. My biggest project so far is Marks Investment Analytics, a finance platform that pulls live market data, backtests portfolios on real historical prices, and runs Monte Carlo simulations to show a range of possible outcomes. Building it end to end taught me how the front end, APIs, databases, and the analytics underneath all fit together in a real product.

Next, I want to build a data pipeline that pulls from public APIs into a cloud data warehouse on a schedule, a machine learning model that makes predictions from real-world data, and a dashboard that turns the results into clear, usable insights. Alongside those projects, I'm teaching myself C++, Linux, and AWS so I'm ready for harder problems. After BYU, I plan to work full-time and earn a master's in Computer Science along the way.

Outside of tech, I'm married and spend my free time building things by hand: 3D printing and designing my own parts, welding, and woodworking. I also play basketball and football, and I'm usually in the middle of a book. A few favorites: The Intelligent Investor, The Psychology of Money, How to Win Friends and Influence People, Walter Isaacson's Steve Jobs, and Brandon Sanderson's Reckoners series.
